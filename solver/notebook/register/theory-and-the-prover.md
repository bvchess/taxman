## The Python heuristics project

The strategies (solvent, onetax, maxturn, cascade, continuation), the certificates and the experiments that did not pay
(a 2-tax rule for OneTax, the fork oracle, trusting certificates as cutoffs, the matroid frame, matching-existence as
acceptance, bitsets) are in [heuristics/README.md, "Dead ends worth
remembering"](../../../heuristics/README.md#dead-ends-worth-remembering), with what is proven, conjectured and measured in
[its table of claims](../../../heuristics/README.md#the-theory-the-upper-half-is-a-matching-problem) and [the theory's
evidence](../../../docs/theory/evidence.md).  Entries that also bear on the solver (the matroid frame, the
single-sacrifice route) have their row above.
