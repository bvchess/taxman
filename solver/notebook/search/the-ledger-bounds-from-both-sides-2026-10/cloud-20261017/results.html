What each spent on the games the ledger proved and on those it left open. On the games it proved, the ledger paid about twice what the chain paid, because the chain had each game's neighbour below already proven; the chain spent nearly all of its cost on the games the ledger left open. The ledger's advantage is reach, not price: it proved those games without first proving everything below them.

What is left open: how much the bounds tightened

For the 349 open games the gap between the bounds shrank from a median 1,644 to 1,175: a median 25% of each game's gap closed. Both bounds moved by similar amounts (medians 170 for the best game, 138 for the upper bound), but the best game started much closer: it is now a median 146 below the optimum (68% closer; the optimum itself in 94 games), while the upper bound is still 1,020 above it (11% closer).
