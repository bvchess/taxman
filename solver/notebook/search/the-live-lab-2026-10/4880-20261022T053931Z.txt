## Estimate

* **The search in flight:** the sum of the fit over the 2,359 units is 4.0 × 10⁷ steps.  Anchoring a slope of 0.344
  per 100 points instead gives 2.8 × 10⁷, and a slope of 0.5 gives 1.1 × 10⁸.  Allowing for the scatter (×1.6 for a
  log-normal of sd 0.42) and for units like 4875-294 that run ten times above the line gives a range of 3 × 10⁷ to
  2 × 10⁸ steps.  At the chain's 42k to 64k steps/s, that is 8 minutes to 1.3 hours.  The 20 largest units hold 66% of
  the predicted sum, and the largest is predicted at 4.9 × 10⁶.
* **Actual:** 31,285,036 steps, about 11 minutes.  That is 0.78 of the point estimate, at the low end of the range.  I
  found the result just after making the estimate, at 05:39Z.  The numbers above were computed before I looked.
* **A fresh search:** after 8 minutes on one thread (189,123 steps), the fresh search's checkpoint
  (`snap/4880-20261022T053906Z`) held 77 units, gaps 5 to 1,950.  The same fit sums to 4.0 × 10⁸ steps, with a range of
  1.6 × 10⁸ to 3.5 × 10⁹ for the two slopes.  That is ten times finishing the search in flight, so a restart was not
  justified.

## Ideas tried

* **Conflict branching off against on**, same units, same start, 120 CPU s each.  Off took 7.5 times on's steps at gap
  599, 7.9 times at 808 and 19 times at 897, and at least 4.8 times at 1,004.  On unit 4880-241 (gap 1,476), off
  finished in 20,396 steps and 30 s, while on had not finished at 49,957 steps.  So on stays, but the choice that is
  best for a single unit is not always the same.  Since the lab can switch branching only for a whole game, I see no
  use for this now.  A portfolio over units would be a new feature, and I have not tested one.
* **A better incumbent:** none found.  The chain's 60 s finder at the event accepted no set.  Both of my fresh searches
  started at 542,420 or below it, and every unit run ended at 542,420.  The proof confirms 542,420 is the best.
* Nothing from the register's closed avenues was retried.

## Actions

None.  The command folder existed, but no command was warranted: the in-flight search was ten times cheaper than a

minutes after the event.

## Open questions

* **The procedure's fresh-search command does not chain.**  `taxman solve 4880 --use-prev ...` without `--out` reported
  "no previous game for n=4880 (the game of 4879 is neither in this run nor in the results file)", and searched with no
  seed and no ceiling.  I reran it with `--out` pointing at a copy of the chain's `results/strict.jsonl` in `work/`.  The
  procedure should say to do that.
* **The ten-minute background limit stops long jobs.**  A background job in the session is stopped after 10 minutes of
  wall time.  That ended both fresh searches before `--time-limit 600` did, and ended the 600 s unit runs before 4880-85
  reported.  Use limits well under 600 s of wall time, or expect a checkpoint written partway through (here one at
  5 to 6 minutes).
* **Should a game whose remaining sum predicts under an hour start a study at all?**  A study cannot act before such a
  game ends.  Here, the 4.0 × 10⁷ estimate could have been made by the hook from the units alone, in seconds.
