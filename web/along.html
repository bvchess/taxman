Along the way

Finding the games turned up some surprises. Each is told in the chapter that explains it.

  • Game 40Three factors of taxAn optimal game can take 30 while 6, 10 and 15 are all still in the pot.
  • Game 833More moves, fewer pointsEvery optimal game makes 362 moves, but some games make 363.
  • Every gameThe cofactor ruleWhether a game needs a search can usually be told from n and its largest prime factor.
  • Game 2873The greedy that shouldn’t workThe usual guarantee for greedy fails here, yet greedy still picks best in every game to 100,000.
  • Game 425Two optimal setsEvery game up to 424 has one optimal set; game 425 has two.
  • Games 1 to 77Exact to 77A bound from linear algebra gives the best score of every game up to 77, and misses first at 78.
  • The whole chainThe player’s shareIn the large games an optimal game wins about 63% of the board, between Hensley’s bounds of 1/2 and 35/48.
